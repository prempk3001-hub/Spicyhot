ABOUT US

Welcome to SpiceHot

SpiceHot is a modern Indian kitchen serving home-style favourites made fresh to order — from fluffy idlis and crisp dosas to slow-cooked biryanis and creamy dal makhani.

We use quality ingredients and traditional spices to bring you the taste of a home-cooked meal, delivered hot to your door in minutes.
